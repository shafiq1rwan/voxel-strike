## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: environment mockup for Sector 5 of VOXELSTRIKE, an original browser voxel FPS.
Primary request: Create one polished wide 16:9 first-person environment concept for a new sector called GEOTHERMAL CORE, a deeper underground power complex reached after the Sector 4 Biofoundry.
Input image: reference/sector4-biofoundry.png is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Preserve its coarse voxel geometry, hard pixel edges, noisy tiled steel textures, box-built props, human-scale architecture, and retro 1990s FPS rendering. Create an entirely new room and lighting identity.
Scene: a cavernous but enclosed geothermal reactor hall excavated into dark blocky basalt. A massive square industrial heat-exchanger tower stands in the middle distance, its orange-hot interior visible through thick rectangular steel ribs. Recessed molten-rock channels run below the floor on either side of a broad safe central metal walkway. Two wide connected flanking routes around steel machinery and substantial square pillars provide clear combat space and cover. Short metal stairs reach a maintenance catwalk on the left; thick rectangular coolant ducts and dark overhead girders frame the ceiling. A heavy security door on the far wall has one small RED keycard reader beside it. Hazard-yellow stripes mark the actual molten-channel edges and hot machinery. Sparse steam from a couple of vents, recognizable square control consoles, worn steel floor plates and riveted panels. No creatures required.
Style/medium: chunky rectilinear voxel 3D game scene with low-resolution nearest-neighbor pixel textures and flat per-face shading. Every machine and rock form must feel buildable from simple cuboids. Pixelated game lighting rather than photorealistic cinematography; retain the reference's pixel density and readable large forms.
Composition/framing: one coherent 16:9 landscape gameplay-eye-level view from the entrance. Floor and ceiling visible. Strong depth and a clear open route toward the far door. A small simple dark gray cuboid sci-fi sidearm with a tiny green sight occupies only the lower right corner. Weapon floats without hands or arms.
Lighting/mood: ominous, hot, deep underground. Orange and amber light from the contained geothermal heat contrasts with restrained electric teal on the coolant consoles and upper work lights. Dark steel #566076, charcoal basalt, near-black #05060a shadows. Keep the floor, routes, and cover clearly readable. Minimal bloom. Yellow only marks hazards; red only marks the locked-door reader; no decorative green lights.
Text (verbatim): small integrated industrial wall sign reading "SECTOR 5" and below it "GEOTHERMAL CORE". No other text.
Constraints: environment design proposal only, original art, no HUD, no interface panels, no title overlay, no watermark, no enemies, no hands, no arms. Avoid rounded pipes, smooth high-poly surfaces, photorealistic rendering, purple biological laboratory lighting, specimen tanks, organic growth, excessive clutter, tiny illegible details, lava covering the traversable walkway, or copied game assets.
