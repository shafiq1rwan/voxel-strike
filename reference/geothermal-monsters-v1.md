This sheet was the concept reference for the monsters now implemented in
the standalone `?sector6` preview. Main views show the proposed silhouettes;
smaller views show attack tells. See [actual models and game captures](geothermal-monsters-in-game-v1.md).

| Monster | Proposed behavior | Player response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinder Hulk | Slow basalt bruiser raises both fists before a ground slam; chest plates expose its hot core during the attack | Move out of the marked impact area, then punish the exposed core during recovery |
| Vent Stalker | Low four-legged ambusher crouches at a vent; glowing back seams brighten before a leap | Sidestep the committed leap and attack while it recovers |
| Kiln Drone | Floating furnace opens two side shutters, charges visibly, and fires a molten projectile | Dodge after its aim locks, then shoot its exposed furnace |

Proposed encounter order: introduce one Kiln Drone across an open bridge,
then a Vent Stalker in a clear service bay, then a Cinder Hulk where the
player has room to move around machinery. Keep the first Power Link puzzle
free of surprise spawns. These are design suggestions, not implemented rules.

Style references: `sector4-vatborn.png` for simple procedural cuboid anatomy;
`sector5-geothermal-core.png` for basalt, furnace heat, rust and teal machinery.
Preserve the sheet's major silhouettes and part counts when modeling, rather
than creating substitutes that only share the colors.
