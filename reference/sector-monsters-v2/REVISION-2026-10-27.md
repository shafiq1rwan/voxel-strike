# Facility monster revision — 27 October 2026

Revised Sentry Bud, Clamp Guard and Wire Leech to follow the selected large models and defining poses in sheets 01–02. These remain procedural box rigs with the game's palette, pixel rendering and existing attack timings.

Screenshots are staged views of actual runtime rigs. They are git-ignored local artifacts, regenerated with `node scripts/review-facility-monsters.mjs` against a running dev server. Concept shading and tiny fittings remain illustrative, as specified in the design contract.

Static geometry is baked at each live animation pivot. Vertex colors preserve the palette and face shading, while glow and moving joints remain independent. The Guard uses the allowed 12-draw ceiling to keep four articulated jaw halves and glowing shoulder pistons; the other rigs are below the 6–10 target range.

Leech targeting now intersects the actual solid boxes under their animated frames, independent of render batching and occlusion detachment. The coil's raised top bar is hittable outside the locomotion collider. Shots through empty space never count as copper. The nearest solid region determines whether a shot damages the coil or the body, with existing world/door obstruction retained. Co-op hit messages carry this region flag to the host and clear it after each client shot.
